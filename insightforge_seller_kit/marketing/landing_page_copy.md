# InsightForge Engineering Edition

## Turn raw engineering CSV/XLSX files into technical reports in minutes.

InsightForge is a self-hosted data-reporting app for engineers, researchers, analysts, and consultants who work with experiment tables, telemetry logs, simulation exports, and lab data.

Upload a CSV or Excel file and instantly get:

- data-quality checks
- missing-value warnings
- duplicate-row detection
- numeric summaries
- strongest correlations
- anomaly candidate rows
- interactive plots
- professional HTML report export
- anomaly-flagged CSV export

## Built for technical work, not generic dashboards

Most BI tools are too heavy for quick engineering analysis. Most AI tools are too generic. InsightForge focuses on the boring but painful work: turning messy measurement tables into clear technical summaries.

## Perfect for

- test-bench data
- sensor logs
- vehicle telemetry
- lab measurements
- simulation result tables
- research datasets
- consulting reports

## Privacy-friendly

Run it locally or on your own server. Your files do not need to be uploaded to a third-party SaaS.

InsightForge supports exploratory analysis. Validate results before using them for final engineering, safety-critical, legal, financial, or regulatory decisions.
